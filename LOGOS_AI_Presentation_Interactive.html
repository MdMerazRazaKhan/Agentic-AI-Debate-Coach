Problem Statement & Motivation
The Rhetorical Training Dilemma
Why traditional solo rehearsal and standard conversational LLMs fall short.
❌ The Rehearsal Friction
  • Mirror Rehearsals Lack Friction: Speakers practice into mirrors with zero dynamic resistance or adversarial rebuttals.
  • Undetected Fallacy Traps: Subtle reasoning flaws (Ad Hominem, Straw Man) go unnoticed until high-stakes competitions.
  • Prosody Disconnect: Speaking cadence, pause frequency, and filler words cannot be calibrated through silent reading.
  • Conventional AI Agreeableness: Standard ChatGPT models are trained to validate and agree, failing to challenge premises.
✔ The LOGOS.AI Solution
  • Autonomous Socratic Sparring: Dedicated opponent personas (The Contrarian, Academic) push back with human-like rigor.
  • Real-Time Fallacy Shield: Instant algorithmic and semantic classification across 8 canonical fallacy frameworks.
  • Live Acoustic Delivery Telemetry: In-browser speech processor measuring pace (target 130-150 WPM) and filler word counts.
  • Longitudinal Mastery Loop: Actionable coaching action plans, classroom assignments, and institutional rubrics.

Engineering Deep Dive
Challenges & Technical Solutions
Solving latency, browser-level audio constraints, and dual-database concurrency.
Challenge 01
LLM Inference Latency
  • Problem: Standard LLM generation took >2.5s, breaking high-stakes debate pacing.
  • Solution: Integrated Groq LPU hardware acceleration, dropping turnaround to <450ms.
  • Resilience: Automatic failover fallback to Google Gemini models ensures zero downtime.
Challenge 02
Browser Audio Codecs
  • Problem: Microphone sample rates and codec permissions varied widely across clients.
  • Solution: Standardized on Web Audio API audio-context buffer streaming.
  • Graceful Fallback: Synthetic mock-prosody simulations maintain full functionality if mic is blocked.
Challenge 03
Dual-DB Concurrency
  • Problem: Concurrently writing to PostgreSQL and MongoDB risked API worker blocking.
  • Solution: Transcripts are flushed via non-blocking background routines.
  • Fault Isolation: MongoDB connection timeouts are safely caught without dropping auth sessions.
